Privacy policy

Website preview notice · 13 September 2026. This describes the current enquiry website. The lodge must confirm its full business privacy practices before a public launch.

Information you choose to share

The enquiry form uses your name, email, optional phone number, dates, guest count and message to prepare an email in your email application. This website does not submit or store these form details on its own server. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself.

When you contact the lodge

Your enquiry will be delivered to buffalopark@gmail.com if you choose to send it. Ask the lodge how it retains, protects and shares correspondence before including sensitive information. Do not include payment card details, identity documents or medical information in the enquiry form.

Cookies and local storage

The site stores your cookie preference on this device for up to six months. Essential hosting and access services may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website. Optional analytics is off by default. No analytics provider is currently connected to this preview.

Optional analytics

If analytics is connected for public launch, this notice must identify the provider, retention period and relevant transfers. The implementation only loads optional analytics after an affirmative choice. You can reject optional analytics or change your choice using Cookie settings in the footer.

External services

Maps opens Google Maps only when you follow the link. Email opens your chosen email application. These services have their own privacy notices.

Your privacy questions

For questions about information sent to the lodge, contact buffalopark@gmail.com or 018 381 2159. You may request access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable law. South African privacy concerns can also be raised with the Information Regulator.

Before public launch

The operator’s legal identity, Information Officer contact, retention periods, service providers and any international transfers must be confirmed and reflected in the final notice.
