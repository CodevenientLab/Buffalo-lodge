BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

A few useful details.

How do I make a booking?

Use the enquiry page to prepare an email with your dates and room preference. The lodge team will confirm availability, the current rate and booking conditions directly. An enquiry does not reserve a room.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is listed as an optional extra. Ask the team to include it in your quote.

Can I book a conference?

Yes. Select Conference on the enquiry page and include your date, number of delegates and catering requirements. The published capacity is 20 to 60 delegates.

Can you accommodate a longer work stay?

Tell the team your dates, number of guests and any work-related requirements so they can advise on a suitable arrangement.
